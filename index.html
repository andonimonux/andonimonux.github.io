03 / ABOUT

At the instrument, and away from it.

An international performer with a particular interest in how musicians learn, rehearse and develop their own sound.

Lessons are not about making everyone play the same way. They are about understanding what a player is trying to do, and finding a more reliable way to get there.

I currently play principal tuba with the Estonian National Theatre ‘Vanemuine’ Symphony Orchestra. My work has also taken me to the Bern Symphony Orchestra Academy, the Spanish National Orchestra and Zurich Opera House. I studied with Perry Hoogendijk at the Conservatorium van Amsterdam and with Anne Jelle Visser at Zürich University of the Arts.

Teaching

Tuba professor at FestiGreu Beneixama (2025); guest masterclass at Soinu Loturak, Basque Country (2025); instructor at the Atrilia Music Course, Navarra (2017).

Who this is for

Later-stage pre-college students, conservatoire musicians, serious soloists, orchestral candidates, professionals and experienced wind-band or amateur-orchestra players.
